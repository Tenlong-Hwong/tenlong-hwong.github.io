About

I'm Tenlong — a Massachusetts-based gamer whose online trail goes back to 2008: a Roblox account, a Gaia Online guild, a YouTube channel of stick-figure animations and Lego movie trailers, and just about every era of PC gaming since.

These days you'll mostly find me on Steam chasing 100% completions, deep in games like Where Winds Meet and Lost Ark, or occasionally streaming on Twitch. I also make the odd Wallpaper Engine wallpaper.

Work

By day I'm a design engineer at RTX (Raytheon Technologies), where I've been since 2023 — cable design, Creo, and Git are the daily toolkit.

I hold a B.S. in Computer Engineering from the University of Massachusetts Lowell (2022).
