Tenata is a personal project, built by one developer — Tenlong Hwong. It is not on the Play Store and there is no company behind it: you install it yourself from the APK below, and everything it does happens on your own phone.

Privacy

Your library never leaves the phone.

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry. Songs, playlists, likes and history stay on your device. Tenata goes online only for the three things that need it:

YouTube

To search for songs and download the audio.

lrclib.net

To find synced lyrics for what you're playing.

SponsorBlock

To find non-music segments so they can be cut.

Features

Everything it does.

The whole list — nothing here is a coming-soon.

Download

  • Paste a YouTube link, or type a song name and tap the suggestions.
  • Picked songs stack into a batch list and download in one go, with a live progress log.
  • Choose the save folder with the phone's own file explorer, plus audio format and quality.
  • Five switches: grab a whole playlist from one link, skip anything downloaded before, even out loudness between songs, cut non-music sections with SponsorBlock, and hold downloads until Wi-Fi.
  • Share a YouTube link to Tenata from any app to start a download.

Home & Library

  • Search your own music as you type, with a one-tap handoff to YouTube search for anything you don't have yet.
  • Shelves for recently played, recently added, most played, and your playlists.
  • Listening stats: plays, time listened, top songs and artists.
  • Library tabs for songs, playlists, albums and artists — each with search and sort.
  • Swipe a song right to queue it, left to like it. Long-press for play-next, queue, add-to-playlist, share, edit details or delete.
  • Play all, shuffle all, and multi-select — including a find-duplicates helper.
  • Playlist import and export as .m3u files.

Player

  • A now-playing bar that drags up into the full player.
  • Artwork pulses with the music, and the waveform is draggable to scrub.
  • Shuffle, repeat, like, playback speed, sleep timer, equalizer and audio effects, an Up Next queue, and configurable transitions between tracks.
  • Synced lyrics found automatically and highlighted line by line, with a drag pill to nudge the timing.
  • Controls from the notification, the lock screen, a home-screen widget, and Android Auto.

And around it

  • Multiple profiles, each with its own playlists, likes and history.
  • Backup and restore of playlists, likes and history to a file.
  • A storage breakdown of what your music takes up.
  • A first-run guided tour of every feature.
  • The app keeps a current copy of its own installer (Tenata.apk) next to your backup — so the folder that holds your music can restore the whole app.

Installing it

  1. Download Tenata.apk on the phone you want it on.
  2. Open the file — from the download notification, or your Files app.
  3. When Android asks, allow your browser or files app to install unknown apps.
  4. Tap Install.

Android warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store. That warning is expected here — Tenata is a personal app you are sideloading yourself.
