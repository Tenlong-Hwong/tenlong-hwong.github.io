Privacy Policy

Tenata Player (com.tenata.player) · by Tenlong Hwong · Effective 29 July 2026

The short version: Tenata Player collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry, and no tracking of any kind. Your music, playlists, likes and listening history stay on your phone.

What the app stores

Playlists, likes, play history, profiles, lyric adjustments and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. If you turn on backups, the app writes a backup file to a folder on your phone that you choose. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — there is no server to upload to.

The one network request it makes

To show synced lyrics, the app queries lrclib.net, a free public lyrics database. That request contains the title, artist and duration of the song being played — nothing else. It carries no account, no advertising identifier, no device identifier, and nothing about the rest of your library. If a song has no lyrics online, nothing further is sent.

Permissions, and why each exists

  • Music and audio — to read the songs already on your phone; that is the app's entire purpose.
  • Notifications — the playback notification with its controls.
  • Audio settings — the reverb effect used by one of the between-song transitions.
  • Foreground service — so music keeps playing with the screen off.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase app data removes everything the app has stored, and uninstalling the app does the same. Backup files you created are ordinary files in your storage and remain yours to keep or delete.

Children

The app is a general-audience music player. It collects no data from anyone, children included.
